## Project Overview

**HoloWaterAI** focuses on improving environmental and drinking water monitoring for Vienna's critical water infrastructure.
Using autonomous holographic microscopy and machine learning, the project identifies light-scattering objects causing turbidity, including sediments, plastics, plant fragments, animals, algae, and bacteria.

The resulting high-resolution object data is linked with upstream environmental changes and operational water parameters to enable better real-time decisions and long-term quality insights.

## Methods & Innovation

- Autonomous **holographic microscopy** for continuous sampling
- **Image analysis** of individual scattering objects
- **AI-supported time-series analysis** for drift and anomaly detection
- **Digital twin** integration for real-time monitoring and decision support
- **IoT-enabled** environmental sensing and data fusion
